Find the right card. Faster.

Card Finder is a 4-question flow that recommends 2–3 credit cards matched to a user's spending and shows a likely-eligibility label before they apply. This page is the PRD, with a working prototype you can try.

Concept only. Built from public information about BankKaro's card comparison business. No internal data. The sample cards are fictional and every number is an assumption or an input you set yourself.

01The problem

Too many cards. Too little clarity.

Cards differ in fees, rewards and eligibility. Users often apply for a card that doesn't fit their spending or that they may be declined for. That means wasted applications, declines and drop-offs before submitting.

How I'd validate this before building
  • 5 short interviews with people who recently applied for a card
  • Session recordings of the current listing page, looking for where users stall
  • Review of decline reasons with partner banks, if shareable
Hypothesis

If users get 2–3 matched cards in under a minute, more of them will start and complete an application, and fewer will be declined.

02Users & goals

Who it's for, and what it isn't.

Primary user

First-time card seekers

Early-career users unsure which card fits their spend.

Secondary user

Upgraders

Existing cardholders who spend heavily on shopping, fuel or travel.

Goals
  • Recommend 2–3 suitable cards
  • Show eligibility likelihood before applying
  • Answer card questions from the card's own terms (Phase 2)
Non-goals (v1)
  • Approving credit
  • Loans and other products
  • Replacing comparison pages

Kill criteria

Scale, fix or kill after 14 days

  • Kill if, after 14 days and the sample in Test design, application starts per visitor have not risen vs. control, or the leading indicator (apply-step submits, complaints) is worse than control.
  • Fix if starts rise but completion is under 70% or the guardrail is breached: pause the ramp and change one thing.
  • Scale if starts rise, the leading indicator holds, and the guardrail stays under 2%. Confirm on decline rate when bank data arrives (assumed 30–45 days); if it is worse, pause the ramp and revisit the eligibility rules.

Thresholds are assumptions to agree with the team before launch.

Phase 1 · MVP

Quiz + rules ranking

50/50 A/B on the card listing page. No chat. Ship if application starts rise and decline rate doesn't worsen.

Phase 2

Grounded card Q&A

Retrieval over card terms, with citations and an "I'm not sure" path. Add only after answer quality is reviewed.
